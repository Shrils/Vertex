1. Installation

Before running any Vertex strategy, you must download the compressed NinjaTrader 8 assembly file and import it directly into your platform. Do not extract the `.zip` file manually.

  1. Log in to your Vertex dashboard and download the strategy `.zip` file.
  2. Open NinjaTrader 8.
  3. From the Control Center, navigate to Tools > Import > NinjaScript Add-On...
  4. Select the downloaded `.zip` file from your computer.
  5. Click OK when prompted by the successful import dialog box.

Scroll down or click timeline to continue.

2. Configuration

Every algorithm is designed to run on a specific instrument and timeframe (e.g., NQ on a 5-Minute chart). Applying it to the wrong parameters will result in suboptimal performance.

  • Open a new chart via New > Chart in the Control Center.
  • Select the designated instrument (e.g., NQ or ES).
  • Set the Data Series timeframe strictly to the one listed on your Vertex dashboard card.
  • Ensure you load enough historical data (at least 30-60 days) so the strategy indicators can calculate properly.

3. Parameters

Once the chart is open, apply the strategy and adjust your risk parameters. While our default parameters are mathematically optimized, you can scale the contract sizing to fit your account size.

  • Right-click the chart and select Strategies.
  • Find the Vertex strategy in the top-left list and double-click to add it to the bottom-left pane.
  • On the right side, review the Properties.
  • Adjust `Order quantity` to your desired contract sizing.
  • Verify that your `Account` is set to the correct simulation or live account.

4. Execution

Activating the strategy gives NinjaTrader permission to place orders automatically on your behalf. Always test new strategies in a simulated account (Sim101) before trading live capital.

  1. In the Strategy Properties window, scroll down to the bottom.
  2. Check the box next to Enabled.
  3. Click Apply and OK.
  4. Look at the top right of your chart. You should see the strategy name followed by a green "True" indicating it is actively running.

Warning: Closing NinjaTrader or disconnecting from your data feed will halt strategy execution. Consider running your platform on a dedicated VPS for 24/5 uptime.
